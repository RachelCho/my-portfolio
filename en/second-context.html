    Why Us

    We understand products, people, and business—together.

    Translation alone won’t get your product used in a new market. When the market and users change, how people work, how they make sense of a product, and how they judge trust all shift too—and teams that built for the Korean market often find these differences hard to see from the inside.

    So good decisions turn into team action.

    Design doesn’t end with screens. We explain design intent and priorities in language the whole team can use, and decide together what happens next.

    1. 01

      Explain the reasons behind priorities using user impact, business importance, and implementation effort.

    2. 02

      Document design intent, requirements, and edge cases before development.

    3. 03

      Break the agreed direction into next tasks the team can act on.

    About Second Context

    Independent product design, shaped by context.

    Second Context is an independent product design studio led by Rachel Cho. As founding designer at a U.S. home healthcare SaaS, she designed an AI scheduling platform, and she has designed operations monitoring and incident management for a global pharmaceutical company and product localization for global companies. She turns complex rules and workflows into product experiences people can understand and act on right away.

    Services

    Start with the help your product needs now.

    We work at the scope your team needs. From a short review to product design and development collaboration, you can start where you are today.

    • 01

      Product strategy

      We look at your target market, users, and business goals together to set product priorities and next steps.

    • 02

      UX research

      We study how target users work and the context they work in through interviews and usability tests, then turn what we learn into evidence you can design from.

    • 03

      Product design

      We design agreed problems as user flows and screens, then carry them through prototyping and development collaboration. Documenting design intent, requirements, and edge cases together reduces differences in interpretation during implementation.

    • 04

      AI-assisted prototyping

      We use AI tools to quickly turn ideas into working prototypes, so your team and users can check them before development begins.

    Approach

    From context to delivery, together.

    We work in four steps, matched to where your product and team are today.

    1. 01

      Understand context

      We look at your product, target market, users, and work environment to understand where things stand.

    2. 02

      Define the problem

      We decide which problems to solve first, and in what order, using user impact, business importance, and implementation effort.

    3. 03

      Design and validate

      We design user flows and screens, then check them with users and your team through prototypes.

    4. 04

      Support delivery

      We document design intent, requirements, and edge cases to reduce differences in interpretation during development.
