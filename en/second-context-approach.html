Our approach

We understand new markets and design the next steps your product will take.

Every engagement starts with understanding your product’s target market and users, and the problems your team needs to solve now. We look at how local users work and what they expect, then turn the gaps we find into improvement priorities and concrete design.

Second Context’s six-step approach tests assumptions about the market and turns them into outcomes your team can act on.

6 steps

Six-step approach

  1. 01Understand the market and context of use
  2. 02Review the product experience and localization assumptions
  3. 03Agree on direction and priorities
  4. 04Design for the local context
  5. 05Plan execution and connect to development
  6. 06Validate and carry into the next iteration

Step-by-step description, deliverables, and expected outcomes

  1. 01

    Step 1: Understand the market and context of use

    Together we define the target market, core users, work environment, and business goals.

    Deliverable
    Market and context-of-use brief
    Expected outcome
    Clarity on the problems to review and the scope of collaboration
  2. 02

    Step 2: Review the product experience and localization assumptions

    We review onboarding, core workflows, navigation, and UI copy, and identify the assumptions to test from a local user’s perspective.

    Deliverable
    UX review and list of localization assumptions
    Expected outcome
    Friction in the product experience and items to validate, identified
  3. 03

    Step 3: Agree on direction and priorities

    We decide which problems and goals to tackle first, based on user impact, business importance, and implementation effort.

    Deliverable
    Priority matrix and design direction
    Expected outcome
    Clear criteria for where to focus your team’s time and resources
  4. 04

    Step 4: Design for the local context

    Focusing on the agreed core problems, we design user flows, information architecture, screens, and copy.

    Deliverable
    Improved user flows, plus designs and prototypes within the agreed scope
    Expected outcome
    Product direction confirmed as a concrete experience
  5. 05

    Step 5: Plan execution and connect to development

    We document design intent, requirements, edge cases to consider, and next tasks, and carry them into development.

    Deliverable
    Design handoff materials and an execution plan
    Expected outcome
    Less uncertainty and fewer misinterpretations during implementation
  6. 06

    Step 6: Validate and carry into the next iteration

    Within the agreed scope, we review user feedback or usage data and define follow-up improvements.

    Deliverable
    Validation results and a follow-up improvement backlog
    Expected outcome
    Evidence-based support for your next product decisions

The overall process and deliverables depend on the scope of the engagement. Short consultations and UX reviews focus on the steps you need; user research, prototyping, development support, and follow-up validation are carried out within a separately agreed scope.
